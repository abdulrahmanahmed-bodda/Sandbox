How to create an order
Follow these steps to create a new shipment order on Bosta:
  1. Log in to your Bosta dashboard at app.bosta.co
  2. Click New Shipment from the top right or the sidebar
  3. Enter the recipient details -- name, phone, and address
  4. Fill in the package details -- weight, dimensions, and COD amount if applicable
  5. Select your preferred pickup date and time slot
  6. Review the order summary and click Confirm Shipment
  7. Your AWB number will be generated -- print the label and attach it to your package
  8. The courier will arrive at your pickup address on the scheduled date
Tip: You can also create bulk orders via CSV upload or through the Bosta API. Check docs.bosta.co for details.
