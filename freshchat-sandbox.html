FAQ

Common questions

How do I integrate Freshchat on my site? +
Click the ⚙ Freshchat tab on the right, paste your widget token, and hit Load Widget. The chat bubble will appear in the bottom corner.
Is there a free trial for Pro? +
Yes — you get 14 days of Pro free, no credit card required. You'll be downgraded to Starter automatically if you don't upgrade.
Can I use this page as a Freshchat sandbox? +
Absolutely. That's the purpose — paste your token in the config panel and the real Freshchat widget will load so you can test flows, bot responses, and routing rules.
What data does the widget collect? +
Only what you configure — name, email, and conversation history. All data is routed through your Freshchat account per their privacy policy.
